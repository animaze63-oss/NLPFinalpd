# Design direction — "cinematic dashboard"

Brief (project-defined):
- **Mood:** a darkened theater — deep blue-black background, a single neon accent, generous negative space, wide letterboxed hero for the selected film.
- **Type:** a condensed display face for titles (self-hosted or a Google Font stylesheet), a highly legible sans for data; tabular numbers for scores.
- **Information order:** film identity → overall verdict → the four aspects (Visuals, Story, Characters, Soundtrack) as equal "reels" → evidence (quotes, words, phrases, people) → methodology.
- **Sentiment encoding:** diverging scale −1…+1 with a neutral midpoint; never colour alone (pair with ▲▼ and labels); colour-blind-safe pair (blue ↔ orange) rather than red ↔ green.
- **Analyzer panel:** feels like a "screening room" drawer; clearly says it runs in the browser and no text leaves the device.
- **Deliverables:** wireframes (mobile + desktop), design tokens (CSS custom properties for both themes), component specs, chart specs, copy for empty/error/loading states.
