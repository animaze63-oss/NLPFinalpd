Architecture

Everything runs on Netlify with no outside servers. During the Netlify build, Python runs NLTK and spaCy over the committed review sample (data/scifi_reviews.csv) and writes static JSON to /data/. The dashboard is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript that reads that JSON. The Analyze your own critique box runs NLTK VADER in your browser with Pyodide in a Web Worker. spaCy never runs in the browser, and no Netlify Functions are used.

Who does what

  • NLTK Punkt sentence splitting (punkt_tab), Treebank word tokens, English stopwords plus a sci-fi stopword list, VADER sentiment per review and per sentence, FreqDist praise and criticism words, bigram and trigram collocations (likelihood ratio, frequency filter) as key phrases.
  • spaCy en_core_web_sm: named entities with an EntityRuler placed before the statistical NER, noun chunks mapped to aspects, lemmas, and the dependency parse that links opinion words to aspect nouns (amod, nsubj + acomp, conj, neg, compound).
  • spaCy + NLTK aspect-based sentiment: spaCy finds a visuals, story, characters or soundtrack mention, and NLTK VADER scores the sentence it appears in.

From product reviews to film reviews

The original project template talked about "brands and products" and noun-chunk aspects such as "battery life". In the film domain the EntityRuler adds STUDIO (Warner Bros., 20th Century Fox, A24), FRANCHISE (Star Wars, Alien, Mad Max) and FILM_TECH (IMAX, 3D, CGI, motion capture) patterns, plus PERSON and WORK_OF_ART patterns seeded from each film's directors, writers, cast and title. Noun-chunk aspects are phrases such as "visual effects", "sound design", "production design", "third act", "world building" and "lead performance", mapped to visuals, story, characters and soundtrack.
