Note on the template wording

The project brief borrowed a product-review template ("brands and products", "battery life"). Here it is translated to film criticism: the spaCy EntityRuler labels studios, franchises and filmmaking technology (STUDIO, FRANCHISE, FILM_TECH) plus people and titles from the film metadata, and noun-chunk aspects are phrases like "visual effects", "sound design", "third act" and "lead performance".
